# Candidate Communication Templates

Ready-to-edit messages for each step of a hiring process, written to be clear, timely and kind.

## Preview
![Templates preview](screenshots/templates-preview.png)

## What's inside
`Candidate-Communication-Templates.docx` has 6 templates:
1. Interview invitation
2. Confirmation and reminder
3. Next steps after an interview
4. Delay update (no decision yet)
5. Rejection after screening
6. Rejection after an interview, with feedback

## How to use
Replace anything in [square brackets], keep your own voice, and send rejections as soon as the decision is made.

## Notes
These templates are generic and are not taken from any employer.
